# 🚀 Content Multiplier

##  THE PROBLEM
Content creators and founders spend hours recording valuable videos and podcasts, but 90% of that value dies inside the transcript. Manual repurposing is **slow, expensive, and draining.** Most people simply don't have the time to turn one video into ten high-quality social posts.

## THE SOLUTION
Content Multiplier is an AI-powered workstation that breathes a second life into your raw data. By injecting your unique "Brand Bible" into a high-performance LLM, the app transforms messy transcripts into polished, platform-specific "bangers" for LinkedIn, Instagram, and X—all in under 60 seconds.

## 🛠 Tech Stack
- Frontend: Next.js 14 (App Router) for speed and SEO.
- Styling: Tailwind CSS for a sleek, professional "Premium Editor" UI.
- Animations: Framer Motion for high-end user experience.
- Intelligence: Google Gemini (LLM Integration) for deep context understanding.
- Icons: Lucide React for clean, semantic visuals.

## 🔑 Key Features
- Brand Identity Memory: Saves your name, title, and "voice" so the AI never sounds like a robot.
- Smart Formatting: Automatically detects platform requirements (e.g., LinkedIn headers vs. X threads).
- The Content Vault: A local history (IndexedDB/LocalStorage) that keeps your drafts safe.
- Premium Editor: An interactive workspace with emoji pickers and live editing.
- Calendar Integration: One-click "Schedule" button to bridge the gap between creation and posting.
